# Platform requirements (checked 2026-10-30)

Recheck a rule against its source before relying on it for a date or a money decision, because these pages change without notice. Update the date above when you do.

## Fee and timing

- The app fee is USD 100 per product, and it is recouped once the product passes USD 1,000 in adjusted gross revenue. [Steam Direct](https://partner.steamgames.com/steamdirect)
- Tax verification can take up to 30 days. [Onboarding](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/onboarding)
- The waiting period after paying the fee is stated as 30 days on the Steam Direct page and 21 days on the onboarding page. Plan on 30.
- A Coming Soon page must be public for at least 2 weeks before release. [Coming Soon](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/coming_soon)
- Store page and build review each take 3 to 5 business days. The build can be submitted only after the store page. [Review process](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/review_process)

The earliest release is therefore the latest of three dates: fee plus 30 days, Coming Soon plus 14 days, and build approval.

## What reviewers check

- Store page: capsules with a readable title, screenshots of gameplay only, no external links, and no unfinished features shown as present.
- Build: it launches on every platform the page lists, and every feature on the page is in it.
